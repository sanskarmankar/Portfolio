1. BTST / Overnight Long-Only Strategy

A systematic ""long-only BTST strategy"" designed to identify overnight gap opportunities from midday-session signals and exit at the following session's open. The strategy has been tested across ""NIFTY 50, NIFTY Midcap Select, NIFTY Next 50, and other Indian indices"".


NIFTY 50 backtest:

946 trades | 59.8% win rate

22.95% CAGR

3.21 Sharpe | 6.20 Sortino

1.90 Profit Factor

-3.81% Maximum Drawdown

6.63% annualized volatility

1.28 average win/loss payoff ratio

The strategy has produced approximately ""25% CAGR on NIFTY Midcap Select and 30%+ CAGR on NIFTY Next 50"" in historical backtests.

The underlying signal can potentially be adapted to ""index futures, directional option selling(credit spreads), and overnight option  buying strategies"". I also tested the strategy across other Indian indices; however, on cash equities, the average trade return is not consistently large enough to overcome overnight transaction costs.


 2. Intraday Directional Strategy

A systematic ""long/short intraday strategy"" designed to capture directional moves in Indian indices. The same framework has been tested across ""NIFTY 50, NIFTY Midcap Select, NIFTY Next 50, and other Indian indices"".


NIFTY 50 backtest:

2,365 trades | 47.1% win rate

31.18% CAGR

2.86 Sharpe | 5.98 Sortino

1.60 Profit Factor

-6.10% Maximum Drawdown

1.80 average win/loss payoff ratio

9.83% annualised volatility

~134 minuets average holding time

The strategy remains profitable on both sides of the market, with long and short trades contributing independently to the overall result.
